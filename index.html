Terms — draft

Use is limited to adults. Content involving minors, non-consensual intimate material, coercion, trafficking, privacy violations, illegal material, and unauthorized copyrighted material is prohibited. Obtain qualified legal review before public launch.

Privacy — draft

The application stores account information, submission metadata and reports in the configured database. Video objects are stored in private object storage. Production deployment should publish a jurisdiction-appropriate privacy policy and retention schedule.

Age & consent policy

Before public launch, implement an auditable performer identity, age-verification and consent-record process, removal and appeal procedures, and all legally required recordkeeping for every jurisdiction served.
